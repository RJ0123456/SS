# Generate Data

## Random number with 1 to 9 digits

Just Generate the adding positive integers.

Some Sample Data:

```code

1 + 1 = ?
1 + 1
= 1 + 1
<EOF>

3 + 5 = ?
3 + 5
= 3 + 5
<EOF>

14 + 2 = ?
14 + 2
= 10 + 4 + 2
= 2 + 4 + 10
<EOF>

24 + 23 = ?
24 + 23
= 20 + 4 + 20 + 3 
= 3 + 4 + 20 + 20
<EOF>

132 + 54 = ?
132 + 54
= 100 + 30 + 2 + 50 + 4
= 2 + 4 + 30 + 50 + 100
<EOF>

61 + 9 = ?
61 + 9
= 60 + 1 + 9
= 1 + 9 + 60 <EOF>

83 + 1 = ?
83 + 1
= 80 + 3 + 1
= 1 + 3 + 80 <EOF>
```

Note: The numbers are arranged from smallest to largest in the last line.
